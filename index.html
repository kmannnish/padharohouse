Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What is Padharo House?

Padharo House is an exclusive single-suite boutique heritage homestay in Amer, Jaipur, directly opposite the Amer Fort. Because there is only one suite, the house, the garden view and your hosts' attention are never shared with other guests.

Where is Padharo House located in Jaipur?

We are in Amer (Amber), about 11 km north of central Jaipur, Rajasthan — right opposite the Amer Fort and roughly 24 km from Jaipur International Airport. Open our Google Maps pin.

How far is Padharo House from Amer Fort?

Hardly a 50 metre walk. The fort is directly opposite the property, so you can be at the gate for first light before the tour coaches arrive. Read our Amer Fort guide.

Does Padharo House have a Jaigarh Fort view?

Yes — the suite looks directly onto the ramparts of Jaigarh Fort, which sit on the ridge above Amer. They are gold at sunrise and floodlit after dark. Read our Jaigarh Fort guide.

How many rooms does Padharo House have?

One. Padharo House is a single private suite that sleeps two comfortably, and up to three with an extra bed — so every booking has the property to itself.

What is there to see near Padharo House?

Every major Amer heritage site sits within about four kilometres: Amer Fort, Jaigarh Fort, Panna Meena ka Kund, the Jagat Shiromani Temple and Sagar Lake.

How do I book Padharo House?

Send your dates through our availability page or message us on WhatsApp at +91 94618 84005. We confirm your dates and the final price personally.
