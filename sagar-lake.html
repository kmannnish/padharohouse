Sagar Lake

Sagar Lake is the historic walled reservoir in the valley behind Amer Fort that once supplied it with water — peaceful, uncrowded and within 4 km of Padharo House.

Take the road that curls behind Amer Fort and the noise simply stops. Sagar — the old walled reservoir that once watered the fort — lies in a fold of the hills with Jaigarh's ramparts standing over it. Almost nobody comes here, which is the whole recommendation.

Where the fort got its water

Sagar is a historic man-made reservoir, built in masonry tiers to catch and hold the monsoon for the palace complex above. Water was lifted and carried up to Amer Fort, supplying its gardens, its cooled pavilions and its household.

What survives is beautifully plain engineering: two linked tanks, broad stone steps running down to the waterline, and small pavilions on the embankment where you can sit with the hills reflected in front of you.

Why we send our guests here

  • ✦For the silence. You are a few minutes from one of India's busiest monuments and you will often be the only visitor.
  • ✦For the wildlife. Peacocks, monkeys on the slopes and a good deal of birdlife around the water, especially at first light and dusk.
  • ✦For the view back up. From the embankment you get Jaigarh Fort and the valley walls in one frame — a photograph very few visitors to Amer come home with.
  • ✦Go in the cool hours, carry your water out with you, and keep your distance from the monkeys. We can tell you the easiest way round from the house.
